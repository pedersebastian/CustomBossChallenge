Build your own boss fight

A BTD6 mod that adds a Boss Creator to the main menu: pick any boss or Elite, change its speed, health and skulls, and play 1 to 10 tiers on the rounds you choose, with every Challenge Editor rule.

Read a share code

Paste a Boss Creator code (it starts with CBC1: or CBC2:) to see the whole setup without opening the game. The code is never sent anywhere: it is read right here in your browser.

Try these

Copy a code, then press Paste in the Boss section of the Boss Creator.

Features

Any boss, any Elite

Bloonarius, Lych, Vortex, Dreadbloon, Phayze, Blastapopoulos and Diamondback, normal or Elite.

1 to 10 tiers

Tiers above 5 are stronger copies of tier 5. Beat the last tier you chose and you win.

Speed and health per tier

Make every tier faster and tougher than the one before, on top of Boss Speed and Boss Health.

Your rounds

Choose the round each tier arrives on. The last tier always gets 20 rounds, like the weekly events.

Skulls your way

Keep the boss's own skulls, space any number evenly, or put them at exact health percentages.

Every editor rule

Map, difficulty, mode, cash, lives, towers, Max Paragons, All Fortified and the rest of the Challenge Editor.

Free retries

Lost? Retry the last round or go back to when the current tier arrived, without Monkey Money.

Share codes and saved setups

Keep up to 50 favourite setups and share any of them as a short code.

Safe for your profile

Custom games are always unranked and are built to leave your records, saves, badges and achievements alone.

Install

Mod Browser (recommended)

  1. Install MelonLoader and BTD6 Mod Helper.
  2. Open the Mod Browser from the Mod Helper menu and search for Custom Boss Challenge.
  3. Download it and restart the game.

Manual

  1. Install MelonLoader and BTD6 Mod Helper.
  2. Download CustomBossChallenge.dll from the releases page.
  3. Put it in the Mods folder of your BTD6 installation.
