Past boss events

Every official Boss Bloon Event since July 2021, with its own rules: the map, mode, cash, lives, towers and the bloon and boss modifiers, normal or Elite. Open one in the Studio, or copy its code and press Paste in the Boss section of the Boss Creator. The mod has the same list in its Past Events row. See the events in numbers on Stats.
